Manual QA checklist

  • Banner appears on first load; Accept, Reject, Manage buttons are all keyboard-reachable (Tab) and show a visible focus ring.
  • Accept / Reject buttons are the same visual size and weight — neither is harder to find or click than the other.
  • Manage preferences opens a panel with both toggles OFF by default (never pre-ticked).
  • Clicking Accept, Reject, or Save preferences logs exactly one ['consent','update', {...}] entry below matching the choice made.
  • After any choice, reloading this page does NOT show the banner again, and the log shows the replayed consent update firing immediately (before this comment's position, i.e. before where GTM would load).
  • "Clear stored consent" button above resets localStorage and reloads so you can re-run the first-visit flow.
  • No inline <script> or onclick="…" anywhere in this file or in consent-*.js — view-source to confirm (strict-CSP requirement).
