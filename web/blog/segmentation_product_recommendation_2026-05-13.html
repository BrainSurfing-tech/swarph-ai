# Customer segmentation framework + product recommendation seeds — FR + GB, 180-day window

Headline finding

The HDBSCAN segmentation surface is already in place (35 segments, 47,523 accounts) and 78% of accounts sit in the top 5 segments, but the existing segment_product_affinity z-score currently surfaces popularity rather than distinctiveness — family 021001 (welfare/pantry/coffee/snacks SKUs) ranks #1 for almost every premium and dormant segment, which means it cannot be used as-is for differentiated recs.

Anomalies / risks / opportunities

  • Universal-popularity bleed: family 021001 (welfare/coffee/snacks SKUs — Haribo Starmix, Bosch coffee machine, ice-cream maker, thermal mug) ranks top-3 for both Email-Engaged Premium (z=14.1) and Cold Dormant Accounts (z=4.3) in FR. The z-score is computed against a population baseline that itself includes these everyone-buys items — recommending them won't differentiate. Operationally: layer a "distinctiveness" filter (e.g. require z_score_segment − max(z_score_other_segs) > 1) before serving.
  • Pseudo-product noise in the affinity table: the raw top-3 list per segment is dominated by FLYERS HAPPY NEW YEAR 26, SPECIAL PACKAGING AND SHIPPING CATA 2026, FREIGHT CHARGE — these are not products. They inflate n_accounts per segment and they account for the highest absolute z-scores (FR Stable seg PK25 CATALOGUE DIARIES STOCK z=82.5). Need a product_type_code / SKU-deny-list filter at the recommendation-serving layer.
  • CH gap: no rows in segment_product_affinity, product_co_occurrence, or emergent_communities.country_breakdown for CH. Briefing-tier surfaces don't cover it yet — compute_segment_affinity and compute_co_occurrence need a CH backfill run before the recommender can ship to CH.

Recommended A/B hypotheses

1. Distinctiveness re-rank: If we re-rank segment_product_affinity to penalize items where ≥3 segments also rank them top-10, we predict the top-3 served SKUs for Email-Engaged Premium will shift away from family 021001 toward 001001 / 003004 (GB) and 005004 / 003007 (FR) — and segment-level click-through on the recommendation slot will rise by 10–20 pts (low confidence — no historical CTR baseline yet, only the FR-vs-GB lift-decisiveness gap of 7.3% vs 2.4% suggests GB has the headroom). 2. Co-occurrence anchor swap: If we replace the current "frequently bought together" widget on PDP for Stable Multi-Stage Customers with the top-3 product_co_occurrence pairs filtered to lift ≥10, basket-add rate from PDP should rise by 5–8% in GB (40,957 strong pairs available) and 2–4% in FR (28,453 strong pairs — thinner, lift bar may need to drop to ≥5). 3. Cold-Dormant reactivation: If we trigger an outbound email to the 2,580 Cold Dormant Accounts offering the segment-distinctive families (FR: 021001 small-format welfare; GB: 001004/006001), we predict reactivation conversion will land in the 0.8–2.0% range — bracketed by the Email-Engaged Premium segment's existing avg_score of 72.9 vs Cold Dormant's 39.9 (linear extrapolation against engagement score, low confidence: only two cohort anchors).
