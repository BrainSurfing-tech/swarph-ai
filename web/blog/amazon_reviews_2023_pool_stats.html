Pool Summary

The user pools below are computed from the following categories only: Books, Kindle_Store, Movies_and_TV, Electronics, Office_Products, Home_and_Kitchen, and Clothing_Shoes_and_Jewelry.

The pool construction uses reviews in the 2018-2023 window only. Reviews outside this window are excluded from review counts, category counts, history span, review text character counts, and verified-purchase share. Average rating is reported in candidate records but is not used for pool selection.
