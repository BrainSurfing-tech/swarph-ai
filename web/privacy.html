3. Data We Collect & Purpose

We collect and process the following categories of data:

A. Analytics & Performance Data

  • What we collect: page views (the page you visit), device and browser signals (browser, operating system, screen size, language), approximate geographic region (country/region), a pseudonymous client identifier used to count unique visits, and session/engagement signals (referrer, session duration). We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name or email — the site has no user accounts or logins.
  • Purpose: To understand how our website is used and to improve the user experience.
  • Tools used: Google Analytics 4 (GA4) via Google Tag Manager.
  • Method: Measurement is governed by Google Consent Mode v2 and is denied by default — no analytics cookies are set and no measurement is sent until you grant consent via our banner. Your IP address is used only to derive a coarse region and is not stored by GA4.
  • Legal Basis: Your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

B. Technical & Essential Data

  • What we collect: Server logs and IP addresses (temporarily, for security).
  • Purpose: To ensure the security, stability, and proper functioning of our website.
  • Legal Basis: Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to collect and store information.

  • Essential Cookies: Strictly necessary for the website to function — including a cookie that stores your consent choice so we can remember it. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: First-party GA4 cookies, set only if you grant explicit consent via our consent banner. We use no third-party advertising cookies.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your data.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("Right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for non-essential tracking at any time (via the "Manage Privacy" control above).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at pierresamson@brainsurfing.tech. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
